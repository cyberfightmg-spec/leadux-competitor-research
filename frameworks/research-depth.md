# Research Depth

## Quick
Use for orientation. Discover the market, validate several key competitors, check current positioning/pricing, and expose major unknowns.

## Standard
Default. Multi-path discovery, Tier A/B/C classification, deep Tier A profiles, VOC, pricing, GTM, verification and red-team.

## Deep
Use for high-value decisions. Broader source coverage, historical snapshots where available, more VOC sources, strategic signals, repeated verification and targeted second-pass research for unresolved high-impact questions.

## Saturation rule
Do not keep browsing only to increase source count. Continue research when a high-priority research question is unresolved or when new independent evidence continues to change the conclusion.
