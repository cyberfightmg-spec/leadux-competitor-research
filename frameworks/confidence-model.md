# Confidence Model

Confidence must reflect evidence quality, not writing confidence.

Assess at least:

- source quality;
- source directness;
- source independence;
- freshness;
- scope match (geography, customer, period, product);
- corroboration;
- contradiction severity.

Prefer labels `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW` in user-facing reports. A numeric internal score is allowed only if the scoring rule is deterministic and documented.

High opportunity potential and high evidence confidence are separate concepts.
