# Research Integrity

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not infer internal CRM/process/follow-up operations without direct evidence.
- Do not treat missing public data as operational weakness.
- Do not equate social engagement with revenue or buying intent.
- Do not equate SEO visibility with market share.
- Do not infer profitability from price alone.
- Do not silently average contradictory market-size estimates.

## Negative results

A negative result is valid when the search space is documented. Preserve it as `NOT_FOUND` with limitations.

## Temporal scope

Current-state research must use sources that are current enough for the claim. Historical evidence can explain trend, but should not be presented as current without verification.
