# Voice of Customer Framework

Use public customer language to understand the market, not to manufacture personas.

Extract:

- pain;
- trigger;
- desired outcome;
- objection;
- switching reason;
- praised capability;
- missing capability;
- buying criterion;
- workaround / alternative;
- exact language.

For each theme track:

- number of mentions;
- source diversity;
- recency;
- intensity;
- customer segment when known.

A single dramatic review is anecdotal evidence, not a market-wide conclusion.
