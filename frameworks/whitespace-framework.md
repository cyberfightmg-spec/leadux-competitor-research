# Competitive Whitespace Framework

Whitespace is not simply "a feature competitors do not have".

Search for:

- audience whitespace;
- workflow whitespace;
- price/packaging whitespace;
- business-model whitespace;
- language/geography whitespace;
- channel/distribution whitespace;
- positioning whitespace;
- service/onboarding whitespace.

A candidate opportunity becomes strategically useful only when all three are supported:

1. demand or meaningful pain exists;
2. competitor coverage is weak or poorly matched;
3. the opportunity is realistically enterable.

If demand evidence is absent, label it a hypothesis, not an opportunity.
