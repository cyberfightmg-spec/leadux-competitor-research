# Source Quality Framework

Score **quality** separately from **relevance** and **independence**.

## Suggested quality tiers

- A — regulator, government, official registry, audited filing, primary dataset.
- B — official company pricing/product/documentation/changelog or first-party announcement.
- C — reputable industry body, established research publication, strong secondary dataset.
- D — review platform, marketplace, public community or social evidence.
- E — aggregator, SEO estimate, unverified commercial estimate.
- F — unclear provenance.

## Independence

Syndicated articles, press-release rewrites and pages citing one original source belong to one source family. Multiple URLs do not automatically mean corroboration.

## Relevance

A high-quality source may still be irrelevant to the exact geography, segment, period or product version. Record scope explicitly.
