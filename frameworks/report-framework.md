# Report Framework

A full report should be layered.

## Executive layer

- research scope and date;
- competitive intensity;
- strongest validated findings;
- most important data gaps;
- top whitespace candidates with confidence;
- where not to compete;
- recommended validation tests.

## Competitive landscape

Direct / indirect / substitute / DIY / adjacent map with reasons and evidence.

## Tier A profiles

Positioning, target, product, pricing, customer evidence, GTM, strategic signals, strengths, weaknesses and threat relevance.

## Cross-competitor comparison

Focus on decision dimensions, not hundreds of feature checkboxes.

## Evidence appendix

For each major conclusion list claim IDs, URLs, source type, date and confidence.
