# Competitor Taxonomy

Classify competitors by the **customer job and budget**, not just category labels.

- `DIRECT` — same core JTBD, similar target customer, competing for the same purchase.
- `INDIRECT` — solves the same underlying job through a materially different product/category.
- `SUBSTITUTE` — alternative solution that can remove the need for the target product.
- `DIY` — spreadsheets, generic tools, internal processes, manual labor or self-built solutions.
- `ADJACENT` — nearby product that could expand into the job or compete for part of the budget.

Every classification must include a reason and confidence.

Do not assume the user's known competitors define the entire market.
