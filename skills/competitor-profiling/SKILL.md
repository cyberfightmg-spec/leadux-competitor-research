# Competitor Profiling

## Inputs
Tier A/B competitors and research questions.

## For Tier A collect, when available
- company/product identity;
- current positioning: headline, promise, target, proof, CTA;
- target segment and use cases;
- product and workflow;
- pricing/packaging;
- integrations/ecosystem;
- onboarding and implementation signals;
- customer evidence;
- GTM/channel footprint;
- changelog/product velocity;
- hiring/partnership/funding/expansion signals;
- important contradictions and unknowns.

## Separation rule
Keep three layers separate:

1. `Observed`: what source explicitly shows.
2. `Interpreted`: what the evidence suggests.
3. `Unknown`: what cannot be established.

## Output
A normalized competitor profile with claim IDs and observed dates. Strengths and weaknesses must cite evidence and should be relative to the user's target segment, not generic adjectives.
