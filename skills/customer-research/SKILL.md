# Customer Research

## Extract
- customer segments;
- JTBD;
- pains and triggers;
- desired outcomes;
- buying criteria;
- objections;
- alternatives/workarounds;
- switching reasons;
- language customers use.

Use first-party customer stories carefully: they are useful for use cases but are selected marketing evidence. Corroborate with independent customer sources where possible.

## Confidence
High: repeated unprompted theme across multiple independent source types/segments.
Medium: repeated in limited sources or one segment.
Low: single source or ambiguous context.

## Output
Evidence-backed segment/JTBD map. Do not invent age, income, title, company size or motivations that were not observed.
