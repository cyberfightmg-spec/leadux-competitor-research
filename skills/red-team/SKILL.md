# Red Team

Try to disprove the leading interpretation.

Ask:
- Why might the strongest competitor be stronger than the profile suggests?
- Could the apparent gap exist because customers do not care?
- Are complaints concentrated in an unprofitable or atypical segment?
- Are we confusing discussion volume with buying intent?
- Could a substitute be the real incumbent?
- Could regulation, integration cost, distribution or switching costs block entry?
- Are we over-weighting highly visible competitors?
- What evidence directly contradicts our recommendation?

## Output
For each conclusion: strongest counterargument, supporting evidence, severity, what would invalidate the thesis, and whether the recommendation survives unchanged / survives with conditions / should be downgraded to hypothesis.
