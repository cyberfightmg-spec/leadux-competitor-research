# Market Monitoring

Use only after baseline profiles exist.

## Monitor Tier A targets
Potential surfaces:
- homepage/positioning;
- pricing;
- product/features/docs;
- changelog/releases;
- jobs;
- partnerships;
- review themes;
- selected social/news signals.

## Meaningful event types
`PRICING_CHANGE`, `POSITIONING_CHANGE`, `PRODUCT_LAUNCH`, `FEATURE_CHANGE`, `NEW_SEGMENT`, `PARTNERSHIP`, `HIRING_SIGNAL`, `FUNDING`, `SENTIMENT_SHIFT`, `NEW_COMPETITOR`.

Do not alert on raw HTML diffs. Normalize the change, compare with baseline, attach evidence and assign importance/confidence.
