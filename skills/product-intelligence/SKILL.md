# Product Intelligence

Investigate product capability beyond checkbox comparison.

## Dimensions
- primary workflows and jobs supported;
- core features;
- AI/automation depth where relevant;
- integrations/API;
- customization;
- onboarding/setup burden;
- mobile/platform support;
- security/enterprise capabilities;
- feature maturity and emphasis;
- product velocity from changelog/releases.

## Rules
- "Not visible" is not "not supported".
- Distinguish announced/beta/planned from generally available.
- Prefer docs/changelog/help center over marketing-page implication.
- Do not score feature counts as product quality.

## Output
For each comparison dimension return evidence, current status, confidence, user relevance, and important unknowns.
