# Voice of Customer

## Sources
Use relevant public reviews, communities, forums, app stores, marketplaces, videos/comments or social discussions when legally accessible.

## Extract each mention
- competitor/product;
- source/date;
- segment when observable;
- sentiment;
- type: pain / praise / request / objection / switching / workaround;
- short quote or faithful paraphrase;
- theme;
- severity/intensity.

## Cluster themes
For each theme calculate/record:
- mentions;
- unique sources;
- source diversity;
- recency;
- intensity;
- affected segment;
- representative evidence.

## Rules
Anecdotes are not prevalence. Avoid claiming "users hate X" from isolated negative reviews. Watch review sampling bias and vendor-response effects.
