# GTM Intelligence

Investigate observable presence and strategy across relevant channels:
- SEO/content;
- paid search/ads if evidence exists;
- social/video;
- communities;
- marketplaces/directories;
- affiliates/referrals;
- partners/integrations;
- events/webinars;
- product-led vs sales-led signals;
- outbound signals where publicly observable.

## Distinguish
`presence` ≠ `performance`.

Do not claim a channel works because content exists. Do not infer CAC, conversion or pipeline from visibility alone.

## Output
Channel map with evidence, activity level, target audience clues, content themes, observable strengths, saturation and unknown performance metrics.
