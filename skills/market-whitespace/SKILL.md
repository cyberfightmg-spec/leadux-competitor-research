# Market Whitespace

## Preconditions
Do not run from competitor feature tables alone. Require customer/demand evidence plus competitor coverage evidence.

## Candidate types
- audience;
- workflow;
- price/packaging;
- business model;
- geography/language;
- channel/distribution;
- positioning;
- service/onboarding.

## Three-gate validation
For every candidate:

1. **Demand** — is there evidence customers care/pay/complain/search/switch?
2. **Coverage** — is existing competitor coverage genuinely weak or mismatched?
3. **Entry** — can a new offer realistically serve it, or are there structural blockers?

## Output
Each opportunity must contain supporting claim IDs for all three gates, risks, confidence, and a concrete validation experiment. If any gate lacks evidence, label the result a hypothesis.
