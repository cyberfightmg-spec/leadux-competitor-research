# Competitor Discovery

## Goal
Find the real choice set available to the customer, not merely the top search results.

## Discovery paths
Use multiple independent paths where relevant:

1. category and problem search;
2. customer-language queries (`alternative to`, `what do you use for`, `recommend`, `how do you handle`);
3. review/category platforms;
4. marketplaces/directories/app stores;
5. SEO/category overlap if available;
6. communities/forums/social discussions;
7. integration/ecosystem references;
8. substitute and DIY discovery.

## Classification
For each candidate return:
- name/domain;
- taxonomy: DIRECT / INDIRECT / SUBSTITUTE / DIY / ADJACENT;
- classification reason;
- target segment evidence;
- geography evidence;
- JTBD overlap;
- confidence;
- source IDs.

## Prioritization
Rank research depth by ICP overlap, JTBD overlap, geography, product/price relevance, visibility and strategic relevance. Assign Tier A/B/C.

## Quality checks
- Search visibility is not market leadership.
- A directory listing alone is weak evidence that the product is active/current.
- Deduplicate brands, product names, resellers and regional domains.
- Do not exclude substitutes simply because they are in another category.
